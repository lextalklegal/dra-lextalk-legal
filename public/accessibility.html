Accessibility Statement

DRA Learning aims to keep the site readable and usable on common desktop and mobile devices, with keyboard-operable controls, clear labels, high-contrast text and layouts that adapt to smaller screens. A language switcher is provided for key website content in Hindi and English.

Known limitations

Some practice questions or source materials may only be available in a particular language, and some third-party assets or links may have accessibility limitations outside our control. Website translation does not mean every question has been translated or reviewed.

Tell us about a barrier

If you cannot read content, use a control, access a document or complete a learning task, contact us and tell us the page and issue. We will review the report and prioritise a reasonable improvement where possible.
