Community Guidelines

Our goal is a supportive learning space for DRA candidates. Everyone should be able to ask questions, learn at their own pace and receive respectful guidance.

Be respectful

  • Speak politely even when you disagree.
  • Do not shame learners for weak English, basic questions, exam scores or background.
  • Do not share another person's contact details, screenshots or personal information without permission.

Share responsibly

  • Distinguish official IIBF notices from personal notes, interpretations and rumours.
  • Do not claim that a mock question is guaranteed to appear in the actual exam.
  • Do not share leaked, confidential, infringing or unauthorised material.
  • Job posts and opportunities should be treated carefully; do not pay a recruiter or share sensitive documents without independent verification.

No spam or unsafe behaviour

No harassment, discriminatory abuse, repeated advertising, scams, impersonation, threats or unauthorised solicitation. Admins may remove content or restrict participation to protect members and the learning environment, subject to applicable law.
