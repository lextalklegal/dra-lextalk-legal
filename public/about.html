About DRA Learning

An educational initiative by Lex Talk Legal

DRA Learning is built for candidates who want to understand DRA exam topics without feeling lost in difficult terminology. We believe nobody should feel embarrassed to ask a basic question or learn one topic at a time.

Our approach

  • Simple explanations: make difficult terms easier to understand, with Hindi and English support where content is available.
  • Practice with feedback: timed MCQs and answer review to help you spot what to revise.
  • Flexible learning: recorded-learning options and scheduled live/revision sessions where available.
  • Support beyond a mock: community information, legal-awareness updates and career pointers where available.

What we are — and are not

DRA Learning is an independent learning resource operated by LEXBOTICS AI MEDIA LLP. It is not the official IIBF website, does not issue certificates and does not guarantee exam results, employment or income. Candidates must verify mandatory training and certification requirements with IIBF and its authorised institutions.
