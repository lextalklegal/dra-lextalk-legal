Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Before you pay: Please get the package name, final price, access period, class schedule and inclusions confirmed in writing on WhatsApp or email. The UPI process is manual and does not automatically activate a course.

1. Before payment

Contact us before sending money if you are unsure about the language, course content, recorded access, class dates, support or validity. Proposed prices on the website may change; only the amount confirmed to you in writing should be paid.

2. Cancellation before access is activated

If you change your mind before any course content, recording, class or membership benefit is provided, write to office.lextalklegal@gmail.com with your name, registered email, package name and payment reference. We will acknowledge the request and review it under applicable law and the details confirmed at purchase.

3. After digital access or a class has been provided

Where digital resources have been accessed, a live session has been attended or another package benefit has been delivered, any refund or adjustment will be assessed considering the service already provided, the written package terms and applicable law. Please contact support promptly if access was not provided as confirmed or if there was a duplicate/incorrect payment.

4. Incorrect or duplicate payment

If you believe you paid the wrong amount or made a duplicate transfer, contact us with the date, amount, UPI transaction reference and screenshot. Do not include your UPI PIN, OTP or banking password. We will verify the transaction with the available payment evidence before confirming the next step.

5. Timelines and payment verification

UPI payments and enrolment are manually reviewed. Sending a screenshot does not itself mean a payment has cleared or that access is active. Our team will confirm the verification outcome and any eligible refund/adjustment by email or WhatsApp. Processing time can depend on the payment channel and bank/provider.

6. How to request cancellation or a refund

Email office.lextalklegal@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 93184 45957. Mention “DRA_WELCOME”, the registered account email, package, payment date, amount and transaction reference. Do not send sensitive banking credentials.

7. Statutory rights

This policy does not remove any consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded. If a written confirmation for a specific package states a more specific cancellation term, read it alongside this policy and applicable law.
